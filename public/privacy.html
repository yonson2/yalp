Privacy Policy — Yalp

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Yalp collects no data about you. That is the whole policy, but in detail:

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No ads.
  • No data of any kind leaves your device.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record a take. The audio is held in memory on your device and is discarded when you close the app or record a new take. It is never transmitted, uploaded, or written to storage.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
